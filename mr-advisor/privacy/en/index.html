Privacy Policy – MR Advisor

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MR Advisor ("the App"), developed by Alessandro Graziani, handles information when you use the application.

1. No Personal Data Collection

MR Advisor is designed with your privacy in mind:

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
  • The App does not require an account, login, or any credit card or financial credentials.
  • All figures you enter into the calculators (points, miles, or currency amounts) are processed strictly on your device in real time and are never transmitted to any external server.
  • The App does not use advertising trackers, profiling tools, or usage analytics.

2. On-Device Storage

The App uses local device storage (UserDefaults) solely to remember your app preferences (such as your selected card country/market). This preference stays exclusively on your device and is automatically removed if you uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services and External Links

  • Partner Data Updates (Firebase Remote Config): To keep public partner lists, conversion ratios, and transfer rules up to date, the App retrieves configuration files using Firebase Remote Config (provided by Google LLC). This request is used strictly to fetch non-personal public configuration data. As with any internet request, Google's servers may temporarily process standard technical network metadata (such as your IP address) in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • External Websites: The App includes optional links to official American Express or loyalty partner websites so you can initiate points transfers. Once you open an external link, you leave the App and are subject to the privacy policy of the destination website.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

5. Independent App Notice

MR Advisor is an independent utility and is not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or officially connected with American Express Company or any of its subsidiaries.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
